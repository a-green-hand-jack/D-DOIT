D-DOIT

Training-free Adaptation of Discrete Diffusion via Doob’s h-Transform

A frozen masked diffusion model can be guided toward high-reward sequences at inference time. D-DOIT turns that idea into a practical sampler for generic black-box rewards.

Jieke WuQijie ZhuWeimin Wu Zeqi YeMinshuo ChenHan Liu

Northwestern University · King Abdullah University of Science and Technology

↗ OpenReview ⌘ Code ↗ arXiv · coming soon

D-DOIT changes the local reverse transition law: propose candidate reveals, estimate their downstream value with one prediction-head pass, and sample according to the resulting Doob weights.

The idea

Adapt the sampling law, keep the backbone fixed.

Reward-guided adaptation is useful for sequence design, but existing approaches either update model parameters, require differentiable rewards, or pay for large particle populations and search procedures. D-DOIT instead treats adaptation as sampling from a reward-tilted distribution induced by the frozen model.

The discrete Doob h-transform gives the exact reference reweighting of categorical token-reveal transitions. D-DOIT makes it executable with a prediction-head h-estimator, finite-candidate selection, and optional late-stage best-of-K refinement—using reward values only.
